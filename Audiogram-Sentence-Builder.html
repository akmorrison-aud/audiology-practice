Writing the sentence

Rising, sloping, precipitously sloping: a [250 Hz degree] [configuration] to [8000 Hz degree] [type] hearing loss.

Flat: a flat [degree] [type] hearing loss, or a flat [250 Hz degree] to [8000 Hz degree] [type] hearing loss when thresholds span two categories.

Notch: normal hearing sensitivity with the exception of a [degree] [type] notch at [frequency], using the frequency with the most severe threshold.

Configuration
Flat
Thresholds within 20 dB of one another across all frequencies.
Rising
Low frequencies are more than 20 dB poorer than high frequencies.
Sloping
High frequencies are more than 20 dB poorer than low frequencies.
Precipitously sloping
Above 1000 Hz, thresholds worsen by 20 dB or more per octave for at least 2 octaves.
Notch
Loss isolated to a few frequencies, with normal hearing above and below.
Type

Conductive: normal bone conduction with an air-bone gap of 15 dB or more.
Sensorineural: air and bone conduction both abnormal, within 10 dB of each other.
Mixed: abnormal bone conduction plus an air-bone gap of 15 dB or more.
